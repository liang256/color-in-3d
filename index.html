The book

What’s inside

  • 80 pages
  • 8.5 × 11 inches
  • 32 coloring pages
  • $9.99 paperback

Thirty-two of the pages are isometric coloring pages. Each of those drawings is followed by a blank page.

It opens by asking you to color one stack with no rules. Then it names seven ways to color, and offers the One Rule Challenge: pick one rule and use it for the whole book.

A page before the drawings asks you to test the paper. If color comes through, keep a spare sheet behind the page. The art is drawn for colored pencils, gel pens, and fine markers.

What your colors say

Which Colorer Are You?

Find the one that feels like what you just did. Or the one you want to try. Color the stack however you feel. No wrong answers.

  1. A The Sage

    Leaves it uncolored. Sees a thousand possibilities. Feels no need to choose one.

  2. B The Dark Mage

    Fills the entire silhouette with black. Swallows every face, every line, and even the third dimension.

  3. C The Iaido Master

    Colors only the faces on the same side. Every stroke has a purpose. Not one more than necessary.

  4. D The Paladin

    One tone for each direction. Light, mid, and dark. Graphic, yet spacious. Protects every side and leaves nothing overlooked.

  5. E The Mage

    Gives every face a different tone or pattern. No two faces alike. No spell cast the same way twice. (Ah, I can’t show the colors, but you can see them, right? After all, you’re a mage.)

  6. F The Happy Bee

    Others see a box. You see yourself, a cute little bee-ing.

  7. G ???

    None of the above. You are free to be anything :)

Who it’s for

Kids, adults, and anyone looking at space

Kids 6+

A coloring page and a visual puzzle: which face comes forward, and which sits back. It is also a book you can sit down and color together.

Adults who want to relax

Orderly shapes, one block at a time. A quiet way to unwind, with the pencil doing the work.

Building spatial skills

A color on one face changes the whole form. Seeing that happen is practice in reading a flat drawing as a solid.
